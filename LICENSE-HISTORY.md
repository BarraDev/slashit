# License history

This repository records its own licensing versioning.

## Revisions before the MPL-2.0 transition were licensed under Apache-2.0

- Public history here is continuous and unrewritten. Revisions of SlashIt on
  the default branch before the license transition were licensed under
  the Apache License, Version 2.0 (Apache-2.0). The Apache-2.0 text member
  of each such revision is its own `LICENSE` file, reachable by reading the
  public history.
- The last revision on the default branch licensed under Apache-2.0 is the
  revision immediately preceding the revision that introduces this file; it
  is the parent of the commit that first carries this page. Its exact commit
  SHA is recorded below after verification.

## Revisions from the transition commit

- The revision that introduces this file is the first default-branch
  revision of the owner-controlled first-party SlashIt material that carries
  the Mozilla Public License, Version 2.0 (MPL-2.0); its license is the
  root `LICENSE` file of that and later revisions. This includes first-party
  files that already existed before the transition, to the extent the
  rightsholder has authority to relicense them.
- Commits on a transition pull-request branch are versioning steps of the
  review; the default branch is the licensing history tracked here.
- MPL-2.0 applies going forward from that revision. Previous revisions'
  licensing terms are a matter of record, evidenced by their own
  `LICENSE` files in the history; any question about what a particular
  historical revision conveyed is answered by the terms under which
  that revision was recorded and by applicable law — not restated here.
- Public repository history is intentionally preserved. Commits before the
  transition are unchanged; their `LICENSE` file is the Apache-2.0 text.
  The current default branch shows MPL-2.0.
